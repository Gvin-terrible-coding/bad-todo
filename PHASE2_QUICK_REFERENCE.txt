# Phase 2 Quick Reference - What Changed

## 🎯 TL;DR - Three Main Additions

### 1. Visual Animations ✨
Battles now have:
- Attack movement (Pokémon moves toward opponent)
- Damage flash (white tint when hit)
- Floating damage numbers (fade out while moving up)
- Smooth HP bar animation (300ms, color-coded)

**Result:** Battles feel alive and satisfying!

### 2. XP & Leveling 📈
- Defeat enemy → gain XP (`level × 20`)
- Accumulate XP → level up (when XP ≥ `100 × level`)
- Level up → stats increase, HP restores
- Messages inform player of progress

**Result:** Pokémon grow stronger, progression feels rewarding!

### 3. Reward Screen 🎁
- After boss battles (wave 5, 10, 15...)
- Choose 1 of 3 random Pokémon
- Recruit to your team
- Continue with larger team

**Result:** Team grows, players can customize their roster!
